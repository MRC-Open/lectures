The Boltzmann Distribution

MRC LECTURE 4 · THERMODYNAMICS FILLS THE WELLS — AND IT IS SOFTMAX
Five candidate antigens, each a well of depth $E_j$ (its binding energy). At thermal equilibrium, the antibody's chance of sitting in well $j$ follows the Boltzmann distribution — deeper well → higher probability. Drag the temperature and watch the wells fill.
$P(j) = \dfrac{\exp(-E_j / k_BT)}{Z}$
▲ Boltzmann probability  ·  ▼ well depth (binding energy)
This is softmax. With score $s_j = -E_j / k_BT$,  $P(j) = \exp(s_j)/\sum_k \exp(s_k)$. The denominator $Z$ is the partition function — exactly the softmax denominator. Cold → the deepest well wins (argmax); hot → every well equally likely (uniform). $k_BT$ is the temperature $\tau$.
