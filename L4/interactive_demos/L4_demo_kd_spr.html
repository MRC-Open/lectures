Kd, ΔG, and the Boltzmann Factor Inside Them

The number every biologist measures — is an energy
The experiment — SPR
Kd = [Ab] [Ag] / [Ab:Ag] = koff / kon
how SPR reads the physics
The rising curve gives the on-rate kon (M⁻¹s⁻¹) — how fast Ab and Ag find each other. The falling curve gives the off-rate koff (s⁻¹) — how fast they let go, set by the barrier out of the energy well. Their ratio is the equilibrium Kd = koff/kon, and ΔG = RT ln Kd — so kinetics on a chip measure the binding free energy directly.
