What Is Free Energy?

MRC LECTURE 4 · THE QUANTITY NATURE MINIMIZES — READ STRAIGHT OFF THE BINDING EVENT
Free energy isn't abstract. It's the running total of what physically happens when an antibody grabs an antigen — contacts form, loops freeze, water is freed. Add them up: if the total ΔG < 0, the antibody binds.  ΔG = ΔH − TΔS, at body temperature (310 K).
The molecular event: 6 CDR loops grip the epitope. Each dashed line is a residue–residue energy Eij — together they make up the ΔH in the ledger.
