Competitive Binding = Softmax

One antibody, a pool of antigens — the denominator is the competition
P(binds j) = e−ΔGj/RT ⁄ Σk e−ΔGk/RT  =  softmax(−ΔG / RT)
apply the Boltzmann factor to every competitor, then normalize — that normalizer is the partition function Z

The denominator Z is the competition: a great binder can still lose to a better one. Softmax, not argmax — the leftover percentage is the physical basis of cross-reactivity and off-target binding.
