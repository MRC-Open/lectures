LECTURE 3 RECAP · FROM TWO ENCODERS TO ONE PHYSICS-PRESCRIBED MODEL
Lecture 3 in one view: two pretrained encoders, pooled to fingerprints, scored by cosine, batched into a matrix, and trained with one loss — which turns out to be the Boltzmann selection probability.
softmax = Boltzmann, so InfoNCE is the physics — the architecture is prescribed, not searched.
$P(\text{bind}) = \dfrac{e^{\,\cos/\tau}}{\sum e^{\,\cos/\tau}} \;\equiv\; \dfrac{e^{-\Delta G/k_BT}}{Z}$
That is why ~4,000–700,000 real pairs suffice across antibody, TF, MHC, CRISPR, miRNA, enzyme and drug domains — not CLIP’s 400 million.
