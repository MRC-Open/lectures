why so little data is enough
Transformer softmax = the Boltzmann distribution of binding (Luce 1959). The identity prescribes the architecture, so training is parameter estimation in a known equation, not function approximation. CALM hits its result on ~100,000× less data than CLIP; every SFM is ~5–7M params, <1 h on one GPU.
direction matters — and it's asymmetric
Each SFM retrieves both ways. The easier direction follows the molecular asymmetry: MHC→peptide (93%) beats peptide→MHC (65%); miRNA→mRNA (98%) beats mRNA→miRNA (25%). The target→agent direction is the discovery direction — given a disease target, retrieve the binder.
