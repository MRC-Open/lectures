Deriving InfoNCE — From Fingerprints to a Loss

MRC LECTURE 3 · WHITEBOARD + MATRIX · IT IS THE BOLTZMANN SELECTION PROBABILITY
Goal — build this
$$\mathcal{L}=-\frac1N\sum_i\log\frac{e^{S_{ii}/\tau}}{\sum_j e^{S_{ij}/\tau}}$$
Step 1 · encode + pool → fingerprints
$\mathbf{q}_i=\text{pool}(\text{Ab}_i),\;\; \mathbf{k}_j=\text{pool}(\text{Ag}_j)$
one vector per molecule (slide 27)
Step 2 · all pairwise cosines → the matrix
$S_{ij}=\cos(\mathbf{q}_i,\mathbf{k}_j)$
every Ab vs every Ag at once (slide 29)
Step 3 · scale by temperature τ
$S_{ij}/\tau$
τ is $k_BT$ — small τ sharpens the choice
Step 4 · softmax a row = Boltzmann selection
$P(j\mid i)=\dfrac{e^{S_{ij}/\tau}}{\sum_k e^{S_{ik}/\tau}}$
the antibody “selects” its target — clonal selection
Step 5 · the right answer is the diagonal
$\mathcal{L}_i=-\log P(i\mid i)$
max-likelihood: maximise P(correct) ⟺ minimise −log
Step 6 · average over the batch  ∎
$\mathcal{L}=\frac1N\sum_i\mathcal{L}_i$
