Deriving the InfoNCE Loss

MRC LECTURE 3 · WHITEBOARD · FROM THE BOLTZMANN SELECTION PROBABILITY
Goal — derive the training objective
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\log\frac{\exp(S_{ii})}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\exp(S_{ij})}$$
An agent (e.g. an antibody) must select its cognate target from a pool of competitors — exactly clonal selection. We do not invent a loss; we write the Boltzmann probability of correct selection and ask the model to maximise it. That single requirement produces InfoNCE.
Step 1 · The Boltzmann selection probability
$$P(f\mid\mathbf{q}) = \frac{\exp(s_f)}{\exp(s_f) + \sum_{i=1}^{M}\exp(s_{c_i})}, \qquad s = \text{sim}(\mathbf{q},\mathbf{k})/\tau$$
Step 2 · Maximum likelihood → negative log-likelihood loss
$$L = \prod_{i=1}^{N} P(f_i\mid\mathbf{q}_i) \;\;\Rightarrow\;\; \mathcal{L} = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\log P(f_i\mid\mathbf{q}_i)$$
Step 3 · In a batch, the competitors are the other targets
$$S_{ij} = \text{sim}(\mathbf{q}_i,\mathbf{k}_j)/\tau, \qquad \text{correct pair} = \text{diagonal } S_{ii}$$
Step 4 · Substitute  ∎
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{InfoNCE}} = -\frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\log\frac{\exp(S_{ii})}{\sum_{j=1}^{N}\exp(S_{ij})}$$
