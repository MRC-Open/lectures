Proof — Temperature Sets the Probability Ratio

MRC LECTURE 1 · WHITEBOARD 5 · STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION
Claim to prove
$$\frac{P_A}{P_B} \;=\; \exp\!\left(\frac{\delta}{\tau}\right), \quad \delta = z_A - z_B$$
Two candidates $A$ and $B$ have scores $z_A$ and $z_B$. Their score difference is $\delta = z_A - z_B$. We will show that the probability ratio depends only on $\delta$ and the temperature $\tau$ — not on the absolute scores, and not on any other candidates in the pool. This is the mathematical heart of "why temperature controls specificity."
Step 1 · Write each probability using the temperature-scaled softmax
$$P_A \;=\; \frac{\exp(z_A/\tau)}{Z}, \qquad P_B \;=\; \frac{\exp(z_B/\tau)}{Z}$$
Step 2 · Take the ratio — the partition function Z cancels
$$\frac{P_A}{P_B} \;=\; \frac{\exp(z_A/\tau)\,/\,Z}{\exp(z_B/\tau)\,/\,Z} \;=\; \frac{\exp(z_A/\tau)}{\exp(z_B/\tau)}$$
Step 3 · Combine the exponentials  ∎
$$\frac{\exp(z_A/\tau)}{\exp(z_B/\tau)} \;=\; \exp\!\left(\frac{z_A - z_B}{\tau}\right) \;=\; \exp\!\left(\frac{\delta}{\tau}\right)$$
