Proof — L2-Normalized Dot Product = Cosine Similarity

MRC LECTURE 1 · WHITEBOARD 3 · STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION
Claim to prove
$$\hat{\mathbf{a}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{b}} \;=\; \cos\theta$$
Let $\hat{\mathbf{a}} = \mathbf{a}/\|\mathbf{a}\|$ and $\hat{\mathbf{b}} = \mathbf{b}/\|\mathbf{b}\|$ be the L2-normalized (unit-length) versions of $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$. We will show that their dot product equals the cosine similarity of the original vectors. This is the identity that justifies why CLIP and CALM L2-normalize their embeddings before computing pairwise similarities.
Step 1 · Substitute the unit vectors
$$\hat{\mathbf{a}} \cdot \hat{\mathbf{b}} \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{a}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|} \cdot \frac{\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|}$$
Step 2 · Pull the scalars out using bilinearity of the dot product
$$\frac{\mathbf{a}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|} \cdot \frac{\mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{b}\|} \;=\; \frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|}$$
Step 3 · Recognise the cosine similarity formula  ∎
$$\frac{\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}}{\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|} \;=\; \mathrm{sim}(\mathbf{a},\mathbf{b}) \;=\; \cos\theta$$
