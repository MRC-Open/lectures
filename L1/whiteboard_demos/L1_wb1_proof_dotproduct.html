Proof — Algebraic = Geometric

MRC LECTURE 1 · WHITEBOARD 1 · STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION
Claim to prove
$$\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} \;=\; \|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\cos\theta$$
Consider vectors $\mathbf{a}$ and $\mathbf{b}$ in $\mathbb{R}^n$ with angle $\theta$ between them. The vector from the tip of $\mathbf{b}$ to the tip of $\mathbf{a}$ is $\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}$. We will show that the algebraic dot product (sum of component products) and the geometric dot product (product of norms times $\cos\theta$) describe the same quantity.
Step 1 · Apply the law of cosines
$$\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\|^2 \;=\; \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2 - 2\,\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\cos\theta$$
Step 2 · Expand the left side using the algebraic definition
$$\|\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}\|^2 \;=\; (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b}) \cdot (\mathbf{a} - \mathbf{b})$$ $$=\; \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{a} \;-\; 2\,(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}) \;+\; \mathbf{b}\cdot\mathbf{b}$$ $$=\; \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 \;-\; 2\,(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}) \;+\; \|\mathbf{b}\|^2$$
Step 3 · Set the two expressions for ‖a − b‖² equal
$$\|\mathbf{a}\|^2 - 2\,(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}) + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2 \;=\; \|\mathbf{a}\|^2 + \|\mathbf{b}\|^2 - 2\,\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\cos\theta$$
Step 4 · Cancel ‖a‖² + ‖b‖² from both sides
$$-2\,(\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b}) \;=\; -2\,\|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\cos\theta$$
Step 5 · Divide by −2   ∎
$$\mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{b} \;=\; \|\mathbf{a}\|\,\|\mathbf{b}\|\,\cos\theta$$
