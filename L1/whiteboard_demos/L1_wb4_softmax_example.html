The Softmax — A Worked Computation

MRC LECTURE 1 · WHITEBOARD 4 · STEP-BY-STEP DERIVATION
Compute softmax for
$$\mathbf{z} \;=\; (2.0,\; 1.0,\; 0.1)$$
The softmax function takes a real-valued score vector and returns a probability distribution: $\mathrm{softmax}(\mathbf{z})_j \;=\; \exp(z_j) \big/ \sum_k \exp(z_k)$. We will compute it step by step for a small example, watch how the exponential amplifies score differences, and verify that the output sums to one.
Step 1 · Exponentiate each score
$$\exp(2.0) \;=\; 7.389$$ $$\exp(1.0) \;=\; 2.718$$ $$\exp(0.1) \;=\; 1.105$$
Step 2 · Compute the partition function (sum of exponentials)
$$Z \;=\; 7.389 \;+\; 2.718 \;+\; 1.105 \;=\; 11.212$$
Step 3 · Divide each exponential by Z
$$P_1 \;=\; \frac{7.389}{11.212} \;=\; 0.659$$ $$P_2 \;=\; \frac{2.718}{11.212} \;=\; 0.242$$ $$P_3 \;=\; \frac{1.105}{11.212} \;=\; 0.099$$
Step 4 · Verify the probabilities sum to 1  ✓
$$P_1 + P_2 + P_3 \;=\; 0.659 + 0.242 + 0.099 \;=\; 1.000$$
