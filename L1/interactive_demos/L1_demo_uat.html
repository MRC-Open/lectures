$y = f(x) = w_0 + \sum_i w_i\,\sigma(a_i x + b_i)$ inside the box is just a big equation — one tunable knob (parameter) per connection
Universal Approximation Theorem A neural network with enough neurons can approximate any continuous function to arbitrary accuracy. Cybenko 1989 · Hornik, Stinchcombe & White 1989
So AI is, at bottom, a giant flexible equation — millions of tunable parameters. "Learning" means adjusting those parameters until the equation fits the data. This is function approximation: discovering an unknown function from examples.
