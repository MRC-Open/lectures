Physics In molecular binding, $\tau = k_B T$ — the thermal energy. At physiological temperature (310 K), $k_B T \approx 0.6$ kcal/mol. Cooling makes selection sharper; heating makes it promiscuous.

AI In transformers, $\tau = \sqrt{d_k}$ (Vaswani et al., 2017). In SFMs, $\tau$ is learned — and the value it converges to should equal $k_B T$ in the appropriate units (Lecture 7).
