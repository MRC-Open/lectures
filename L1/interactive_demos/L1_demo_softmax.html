Why exp? The exponential maps any real number to a positive number — that's what guarantees the output probabilities are positive. The exponential also amplifies differences: a score gap of 1 becomes a probability ratio of $e \approx 2.7$, a gap of 2 becomes $e^2 \approx 7.4$. This is why softmax behaves like a "soft argmax" — it concentrates on the winner without ignoring the rest.
