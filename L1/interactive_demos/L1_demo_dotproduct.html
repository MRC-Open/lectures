Think of a as the feature vector of an antibody's CDRs and b as the feature vector of a candidate antigen's epitope. The dot product is the compatibility score — positive when matching, zero when unrelated, negative when conflicting. The dotted line is the projection of b onto the direction of a; the dot product equals ‖a‖ times that projection length.
