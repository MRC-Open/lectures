You can predict any value from the line — but to pin the slope you need points that span the range, not many points in one spot. Coverage, not count. This is the SFM scaling law in miniature: recover training pairs that span the recognition space, and the whole line — every prediction — follows. CALM's ~4,000 pairs work because they are diverse, not because they are many.
