The Attention Idea

Bahdanau et al. 2015 → Vaswani et al. 2017
  • Don't compress the input into one vector — let each position look at all others
  • Compute a relevance score with every position, weight by relevance, sum
  • The transformer move: remove recurrence entirely; every token attends to every token, in parallel
  • Consequences: full GPU parallelization · no degradation over distance · predictable scaling → the foundation-model era

The Attention Equation — Three Stages

Each stage is one operation from Session 1
$$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) = \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right)V$$
  • Stage 1 — $QK^\top$: all pairwise compatibility scores (matrix multiplication)
  • Stage 2 — softmax$(\cdot/\sqrt{d_k})$: scores → probabilities (temperature softmax)
  • Stage 3 — $\times V$: aggregate information weighted by attention (weighted sum)
