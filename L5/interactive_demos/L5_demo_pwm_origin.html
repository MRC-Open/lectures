The PWM Is the Binding-Energy Table

Counting a TF's sites measures its per-base energies — so ΔG becomes a dot product
known binding sites (aligned)

→
the weight matrix (PWM)
A TF's bound sites are a Boltzmann sample — a tighter base (lower ΔG) appears exponentially more often. So counting frequencies measures energies:
fp(b)  ∝  e−ΔGp(b) / kBT   ⇒   wp(b) ≡ −ΔGp(b) / kBT = log fp(b) + const
So  −ΔGsite = Σp wp(basep) = tf · dnaj  — the PWM is the energy table; ΔG is the dot product.
derived by Berg & von Hippel, J. Mol. Biol. 193:723 (1987)  ·  standardised in Stormo, Bioinformatics 16:16 (2000)
