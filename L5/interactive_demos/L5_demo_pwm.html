① binding follows Boltzmann over sites
P(site j) = exp(−Ej/kBT) ⁄ Σ exp(−Eℓ/kBT)
② substitute the dot product   Ej = − tf · dnaj
P(site j) = exp(tf·dnaj/kBT) ⁄ Σ exp(tf·dnaℓ/kBT)
③ relabel   tf → q,  dnaj → kj,  kBT → τ
P(j) = exp(q·kj/τ) ⁄ Σ exp(q·kℓ/τ)  = the transformer attention equation
