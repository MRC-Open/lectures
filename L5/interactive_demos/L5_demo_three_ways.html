Why SFMs Exist

The landscape is the law — but you can only get it three ways
① MEASURE
Measure it
SPR · ITC · BLI — one ΔG at a time, on the bench
rigorous, but hopelessly sparse
② COMPUTE
Compute it
exactly — but only where the physics is given (nucleic acids)
exact, but DNA/RNA only
③ LEARN
Learn it
an approximate landscape from data — for the proteins you can't compute
the SFM
the resolutionAn SFM is option ③: it learns the binding-energy landscape you could never fully measure or compute. Given-bilinearity domains converge fast on little data; emergent domains lean on pretrained encoders — but it is the same machine either way.
