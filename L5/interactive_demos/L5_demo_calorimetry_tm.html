The Tm You Already Trust Is a Dot Product

What calorimetry measures → where the numbers come from → the dot product
① calorimetry measures the energy
Heat the duplex; the heat it absorbs as it melts is ΔH. The melt midpoint is Tm, giving ΔS = ΔH/Tm, so ΔG = ΔH − TΔS. A direct energy measurement — heat is energy (1st law), no model, no fit. SantaLucia measured this for every nearest-neighbour pair — those ΔG° values fill the matrix below.
② Your sequence → a vector. Each base pair is held by stacking on its neighbour, so a duplex's energy is the sum over every overlapping 2-base step (a "nearest neighbour"). Tally the 5 steps → a count of each neighbour-type.
