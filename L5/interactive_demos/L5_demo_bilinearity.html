Does the Binding Energy Decompose as a Dot Product?

Lecture 4 left ΔG abstract — the identity needs it to be a dot product
Attention scores with a dot product: sj = q·kj / τ. For the identity to hold, the binding energy must be a dot product.
It is — in one of two ways.
① Given bilinearity
the dot product is handed to us by physics
E = −qphys · kphys
  • Interpretable physical features — nucleotide × position
  • Parameters are tabulated, measured once — the form is not learned
  • The energy decomposes exactly, by position, in raw sequence space
Where: TF–DNA (PWM) · DNA/RNA duplex (SantaLucia) · CRISPR guide–target
② Emergent bilinearity
the SFM learns the dot product
E ≈ −Encoder(A) · Encoder(T)
  • Abstract learned coordinates — no physical meaning per axis
  • The encoder discovers the representation from data
  • The energy decomposes only approximately, in a learned space
Where: antibody–antigen · TCR–pMHC · enzyme–substrate · drug–target
Both feed the same convergence equation. Part B starts with the case where the dot product is exact and visible — TF–DNA.
