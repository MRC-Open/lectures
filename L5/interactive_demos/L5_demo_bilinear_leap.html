Why Recognition Must Be a Dot Product

DNA you can measure · everything else obeys the same law — so bilinearity is almost forced
a compatibility score is a dot product of two feature vectors
Binding free energy is a sum of interaction terms → a sum of pairwise terms is a dot product → so recognition is bilinear: ΔG ≈ − q·k

DNA–DNA — you can see it

the components are measured, one by one
  • Calorimetry reads off every per-step energy directly
  • The energy decomposes exactly — contacts independent
  • Look the energies up and sum (slide 17)
ΔG = counts · energies(measured)
given bilinearity

Proteins — you must learn it

the same law, but the components are hidden
  • You can't calorimeter every antibody–antigen pair
  • Contacts are not independent (epistasis) — no fixed table
  • So learn a vector for each molecule's landscape
ΔG ≈ − q · k(learned)
emergent bilinearity
Bilinearity is not a modelling choice we impose — it is the logical shadow of additive binding energy. DNA lets us read the components off a calorimeter; for everything else we represent the energy landscape and take the dot product. Same form — measured for DNA, learned for the rest.
