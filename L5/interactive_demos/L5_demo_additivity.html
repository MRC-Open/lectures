These are real, measured energies (kcal/mol) — not abstract scores.
Yes — they are exactly measured. The total ΔG of any site comes straight from SPR/ITC (last slide). The per-position energies are measured too: SantaLucia obtained the DNA-duplex values by calorimetry — real thermodynamics, no model. The PWM (next) is just a convenient way to read the same energies from sequences.
Why proteins are harder (later). In antibody–antigen binding the contacts are not independent — one residue's effect depends on its neighbours (epistasis). The energy will not decompose by position, so it must be learned.
