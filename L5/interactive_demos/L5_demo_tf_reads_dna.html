How a Transcription Factor Reads DNA

Recognition is position-specific and base-specific
It reads bases, not shape alone
In the major groove, TF side chains make base-specific contacts — H-bonds that distinguish A from G from C from T.
Each position has a preference
At every position the TF prefers certain bases. Together these preferences define its motif.
Preference = energy
A preferred base binds more tightly (lower ΔG); a disfavored base, more weakly. Each position contributes its own energy.
So a TF's recognition can be summarized position by position — the idea behind the position weight matrix we build next.
