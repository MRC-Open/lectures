Affinity Calibration — From Rankings to Real ΔG

Two numbers turn a contrastive score into kcal/mol
a handful of measured Kd  calibrate the whole scale
sij = −ΔGij / kBT + constant
The contrastive score s = q·k already is the binding energy — up to a constant. That constant cancels in the softmax, so ranking needs no measured affinity. But to read off real kcal/mol, calibrate it out:
ΔGpred = w · s + b
w
slope — unit conversion to kcal/mol (≈ −kBT = −0.62 if perfectly scaled)
b
intercept — the thermodynamic reference state (the unbound baseline)
The decoupling principle. The energy landscape is learned from cheap binary binding pairs (thousands → millions). The unit conversion needs only ~500 measured Kd (SPR/ITC/BLI) — two numbers, fixed cost. Leverage ratio: 8 : 1 at 4k pairs → 2,000 : 1 at 1M.
Rankings come free from binary data; absolute affinity costs just two calibrated numbers — w and b.
▸ area of future workAffinity-labelled data (measured Kd) is scarce, and calibration across domains is largely unexplored — a strong SFM student-project direction.
