Deriving Scaled Dot-Product Attention

MRC LECTURE 2 · WHITEBOARD · THREE STAGES, THREE OPERATIONS FROM LECTURE 1
Goal — build this equation
$$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) \;=\; \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$
Each of $n$ tokens carries three learned vectors: a query $\mathbf{q}_i$ ("what am I looking for?"), a key $\mathbf{k}_i$ ("what do I offer?"), and a value $\mathbf{v}_i$ ("what information do I carry?"). Stacked as rows, they form matrices $Q, K, V$. We build attention in three stages — each one an operation from Lecture 1.
Stage 1 · All pairwise compatibility scores (matrix multiplication)
$$S = QK^\top \in \mathbb{R}^{n\times n}, \qquad S_{ij} = \sum_{l=1}^{d_k} Q_{il}K_{jl} = \mathbf{q}_i \cdot \mathbf{k}_j$$
Stage 2a · Scale by √dₖ (the temperature)
$$\tilde{S} = \frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}$$
Stage 2b · Softmax each row → a probability distribution
$$A = \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right), \qquad A_{ij} = \frac{\exp(\mathbf{q}_i\cdot\mathbf{k}_j/\sqrt{d_k})}{\sum_{k}\exp(\mathbf{q}_i\cdot\mathbf{k}_k/\sqrt{d_k})}$$
Stage 3 · Aggregate values, weighted by attention (weighted sum)
$$O = AV, \qquad \mathbf{o}_i = \sum_{j=1}^{n} A_{ij}\,\mathbf{v}_j$$
Assemble the three stages  ∎
$$\text{Attention}(Q,K,V) \;=\; \text{softmax}\!\left(\frac{QK^\top}{\sqrt{d_k}}\right) V$$
