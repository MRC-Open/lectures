Final project — build your own SFM

After Session 4, each of you builds your own Specificity Foundation Model on your own data, using Claude Code with the mrc-sfm-template as the starting scaffold. The finished project is a real, citable research artifact — equivalent to dtSFM or GenLoop — with a Zenodo DOI, Hugging Face weights, and a permanent home in the MRC-Open community. See Section 6 of the syllabus for the full infrastructure and the publication / patent path.

Anyone in the lab can take this course. The math is at the level of college algebra and basic calculus. You do not need to know how to code — that is what Claude Code is for. The only thing you need is your own domain expertise and your own data.
