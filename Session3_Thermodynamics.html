Today's Question

What is the probability that a molecule selects a specific target?
  • An antibody encounters a pool of candidate antigens
  • Each pair has a binding free energy $\Delta G$
  • Question: what is the probability the antibody selects antigen $j$?
  • Answer: the Boltzmann distribution — from the second law of thermodynamics
  • Punchline: the answer is softmax — derived in 1877, used by transformers since 2017
